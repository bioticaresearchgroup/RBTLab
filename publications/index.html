Bismuth/Fe3O4 in situ metal organic framework nanocomposite for image guided magnetic hyperthermia therapy
Liz Hannah George, S. Prathapan, Nishad K․ V․, Manoj Komath, G.S. Sailaja
Materials Research Bulletin  ·  01 Jun 2025  ·  doi:10.1016/j.materresbull.2025.113339
We present a bismuth/Fe3O4 metal-organic framework (BiTP/Fe3O4) nanocomposite designed for synergistic, image-guided cancer therapy. This system integrates CT imaging-leveraging the high radiopacity of bismuth (2981.8 HU) with magnetic hyperthermia via surface-anchored Fe3O4 nanoparticles (reaching 42.1°C within 7 min). The nanocomposite demonstrates excellent cytocompatibility, with Prussian blue staining confirming preferential uptake in human osteosarcoma cells, validating its potential for targeted magnetic hyperthermia applications.
