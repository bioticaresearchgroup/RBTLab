## Lesson
- Never push a Gemfile.lock regenerated on a NEWER local Ruby (3.3) to a repo
  whose CI pins OLDER Ruby (3.1): bundle resolves newer gem versions that fail
  on the runner. Keep lockfiles in sync with the CI Ruby; regenerate with the
  same Ruby as CI, or restore the committed one.

## Lesson
- Google truncates meta descriptions > ~160 chars, so the effective limit is
